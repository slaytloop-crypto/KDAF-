ABOUT KDAF

Company History and Background

KDAF (Kingsley De Alcatraz Food) began as a barangay food creation concept centered around kitchen cooking known in our local barangay. Sharing posts on Facebook helps locals recognize the shop and keep up with available dishes.

Mission

To prepare flavorful, freshly made barangay food creations with care and consistency for our local community in Liloan, Catarman.

Vision

To grow KDAF into a trusted neighborhood kitchen creation brand recognized across the barangay and online community for quality food and friendly service.
