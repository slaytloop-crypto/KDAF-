Company Introduction

Welcome to KDAF (Kingsley De Alcatraz Food)! Our business focuses on homemade kitchen creations prepared for neighbors and customers in our local barangay. We also share regular food posts and updates on Facebook so local residents can easily see what we prepare.

Business Overview

KDAF is a local barangay food venture located in Liloan, Catarman (at Petron), owned and managed by Kingsley L. Ariño. We prepare freshly cooked dishes, platters, rice meals, snacks, and kitchen creations for everyday meals and local orders.

Purpose of the Company

The purpose of KDAF is to provide flavorful, freshly cooked barangay food creations from our kitchen to the local community while keeping customers updated through our Facebook page.
