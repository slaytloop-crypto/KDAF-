FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

  1. What does KDAF stand for?
    KDAF stands for Kingsley De Alcatraz Food, a barangay kitchen creation business owned by Kingsley L. Ariño.

  2. Where is KDAF located?
    We are located in Liloan, Catarman (at Petron).

  3. What dishes are offered on the KDAF menu?
    Our menu includes Sisig, Lechon Kawali Sisig Platter, Pork Tonkatsu, Japanese Panko, Korean Spicy Buldak, Chicken Loaded Fries, Chicken Caesar Salad, Classic Chicken Burger, Wings Platter, Chicken Tonkatsu Wings Rice Meal, and Deep Fried Squid Rings.
